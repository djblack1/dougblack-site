Audi: Plugging In

Concept, narrative construction, and voiceover for a branded documentary on Audi's first plug-in hybrid.

Plugging In, the full documentary.
  • RoleConcept development, narrative construction, voiceover script
  • Agency1000% / Milk Studios
  • FormatBranded documentary film

Audi was launching its first plug-in hybrid, and the brief was to make people feel something about an electric plug. The answer was fifty years older than the car: a folk festival in Newport, Rhode Island, where a young musician walked onstage with an electric guitar and got booed for it.

Most of the film is constructed from interviews: people who were at Newport in 1965, alongside Courtney Barnett, Jim James, and Colin Meloy speaking to what that act of defiance meant across the decades. I built the narrative out of those transcripts, then wrote the voiceover that carried between them.

Google for Work

Concept and scripts for digital spots connecting IT professionals to the people they support.

Android for Work: Choice, Foundation, and Impact.
  • RoleConcept, script
  • AgencyStink Studios
  • FormatDigital spots, B2B

B2B technology advertising usually addresses the buyer and forgets the user. The idea here was to invert it: build each spot around someone doing physical, industrious work, and let the Android device sit at the center of it without becoming the subject. The audience was IT professionals, and the argument was about the people they serve.

SoundCloud Go

Launch films, social, and campaign copy for SoundCloud's first subscription.

SoundCloud Go, campaign film.
  • RoleBrand voice, launch copy, scripts
  • ClientSoundCloud
  • FormatFilm, OOH, social, in-product

The films had to feel native to SoundCloud itself: energetic, a little chaotic, unapologetically music-obsessed. Nothing polished enough to announce that a marketing department made it.

Why it works

Every line is a range, and every range is a joke that only lands if you know both ends of it. The campaign's argument was that the platform contained multitudes, so the copy had to prove it five words at a time rather than claim it in a sentence.

Intel and Milk Studios: Adorned

Creative direction and scripts for a film series on the history of wearable technology, shown at the Adorned exhibition at Milk Gallery.

The Things We Wear: Exoskeletons.
The Things We Wear: Watches.
The Things We Wear: Glasses.
  • RoleCreative direction, scripts
  • AgencyMilk Studios
  • FormatExhibition film series

Intel and Milk built MUZSE around the point where technology meets fashion, and these films had to work in a gallery rather than a feed. No scroll, no skip, an audience standing still and choosing to watch. That changes what a script can do. It can be slower, and it has to be worth the standing.

Pantone Connect

Three studio films for the launch of Pantone’s color platform, each one scripted from a free-flowing interview rather than written as lines to read.

  • RoleCreative direction, interviews, and scripts, live event script
  • AgencyPresent
  • FormatSocial cutdowns, from a live launch broadcast

Rather than just detailing the features of Pantone Connect, we put it in the hands of working artists instead. I interviewed each of them in their studio, so the scripts were open questions built from those conversations. Every film had to reach the same three points: where the color comes from, what happens to it in the design, and how it survives production.

These are the forty-second social cutdowns. Each one ends on an invitation to record a question for the artist, which they answered live on the broadcast.
