How I Work

Writing for readers who aren't people.

Every line now has to work for a potential customer, a search engine, and the model deciding whether your brand is worth citing. Most of what gets sold as AEO and GEO is a brand voice problem dressed up as a technical one. A model cites only what it can extract cleanly and sees consistently stated everywhere it looks. Which means precise, authoritative writing on every touchpoint doesn't just carry the message, it recruits the messengers.

Building a voice where none exists.

It starts with reading everything the brand has ever published, then finding the three sentences it keeps trying to write. I build the system that lets it finish them. It's both simpler and harder than it sounds.

I used to interview people for a living.

It's the best preparation for sitting across from a CMO. I get the business problem first. The writing comes second.

The best compliance copy doesn't sound like it.

Years of working in financial services taught me the discipline. The copy that moves a Goldman or AmEx product is the copy that doesn't get cut by legal. It's underrated work.
