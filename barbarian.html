American Express

Member Because

Cardmember acquisition campaign  ·  With Huge  ·  Sole writer
Silver, Best Mobile Marketing Campaign, NYC Design Awards

Insight
Marketing does nothing for someone choosing between twenty cards. People believe other members.
Idea
Member Because. Real cardmembers explain why they love their card, in their own words.
How it showed up
I built the personas from cardmember interviews. Their faces, names, and words ran inside the shopping flow, next to the card each one carries.
My role
Sole writer.

“Real Cardmembers. Real Reviews.”

The campaign line

Why it’s here

Membership is most persuasive when the voice is from members.

Pantone Connect

Meet the artists at work

Launch campaign  ·  With Present  ·  November 2021

Insight
Inspiration doesn’t wait for a designer to get back to the studio. When it strikes, the swatch book is somewhere else.
Idea
Follow three New York artists and show how they use Pantone Connect to catch color wherever it appears: an ATM, a traffic cone, a weathered stool.
How it showed up
Three artist films across social, email, and the microsite. Each ended by inviting viewers to send the artist a video question, answered at the live-streamed launch.
Result
The campaign anchored Pantone’s first virtual launch event built for the design community.
My role
I helped conceive the launch, interviewed the artists, and wrote the films, the social, the microsite, and the script for the live broadcast.

04

SoundCloud Go

Online and ad-free

Launch campaign  ·  SoundCloud  ·  First dedicated writer

Insight
SoundCloud listeners are connoisseurs of genres most services can’t touch. Celebrate their taste before asking them to subscribe.
Idea
Lead with the music, not the feature list. Each line pairs two genres and ends on the same promise.

From freestyle to free jazz.

From 8-bit to 2step.

From yacht rock to pirate metal.

Online and ad-free.

The launch film, made to look and feel like something that could live on SoundCloud.

How it showed up
Every social channel, plus video, email, in-product, out of home, and audio.
The stakes
SoundCloud’s first paid subscription, pitched to 175 million listeners who had never paid.
My role
I wrote the launch and the voice behind it, and argued for the insight over a feature list.

Why it’s here

One insight that writes an infinitely interactive line for every genre, in any channel.
